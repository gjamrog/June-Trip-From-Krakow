01

Solina & Bieszczady Mountains

🚗 Drive from Kraków: ~3.5 h · 180 km
Poland's wild heart.
The Bieszczady's grassy ridges roll on for miles, dotted with sheep and wooden churches. Solina Lake — the largest in Poland — mirrors it all. Boat trips, campfire evenings, and trails that feel a world away.

Thursday 18 June

  • Depart Kraków ~1:00 PM, scenic drive south-east through Pogórze Przemyskie
  • Arrive Solina ~4:30 PM, check into cottage
  • Walk across the Solina Dam — Poland's largest water dam, 664 m long
  • Rent a motorboat or kayak and explore the lake's coves and inlets
  • Sunset photo session on the eastern shore
🍽 Dinner at Restauracja Poloninka with lake view

Friday 19 June

  • Early start ~7:00 AM, drive to Ustrzyki Górne trailhead
  • Hike to Tarnica (1,346 m) — the highest Bieszczady peak, ~5 h round trip
  • Ridge-line views stretching across Poland, Slovakia, and Ukraine
  • Picnic lunch on the summit meadows
  • Descend via Połonina Caryńska for a different panorama
🥪 Pack picnic lunch · 🍽 Dinner at the cottage's outdoor grill

Saturday 20 June

  • Leisurely morning, then hike Połonina Wetlińska (easier ridge, ~3 h)
  • Visit Chatka Puchatka — the famous mountain shelter at 1,228 m
  • Horse-drawn cart ride through the valley meadows
  • Afternoon: visit the Museum of Bieszczady History in Ustrzyki Górne
  • Evening campfire with oscypek (smoked cheese) and grilled kiełbasa
🍽 Lunch at Chatka Puchatka · 🍽 Bonfire dinner by the lake

Sunday 21 June

  • 6:00 AM — Depart Solina (pack the car the night before)
  • Breakfast on the road or packed from the cottage
  • 9:30 AM — Arrive Kraków, drop off car
  • 10:00 AM — Back home as planned
🥡 Breakfast on the road
🛌 Where to sleep: Pensjonat pod Gwiazdami or Domki nad Soliną (self-catering cottages for 4). Book early — June fills up fast!
02

Szczawnica & Pieniny Mountains

🚗 Drive from Kraków: ~1.5 h · 100 km
The closest mountain escape.
A charming spa town at the foot of sheer limestone cliffs. The Dunajec River Gorge raft trip is a national treasure — wooden rafts, towering rock walls, and castles overhead. Trzy Korony peak awaits hikers.

Thursday 18 June

  • Depart Kraków ~3:00 PM, easy drive south
  • Arrive Szczawnica ~4:30 PM, check into hotel
  • Evening stroll on the Zdrojowy Promenade — beautiful park with music pavilion
  • Walk to the Szczawnica Pier for a view of the Dunajec valley
  • Try the local mineral water from the drinking fountain
🍽 Dinner at Karczma u Pienina — regional pierogi and grilled trout

Friday 19 June

  • 9:00 AM — Drive to Sromowce Wyżne (15 min), board traditional wooden raft
  • Dunajec River Gorge rafting — 2 h float through breathtaking limestone canyon
  • Arrive in Szczawnica, walk back along the river trail
  • Afternoon: hike to Trzy Korony viewpoint (982 m) — the three-crowned peak
  • Panorama of the Tatras, Pieniny, and Dunajec meandering below
🥪 Lunch in Szczawnica's market square · 🍽 Dinner at Karczma Góral

Saturday 20 June

  • Morning: hike to Sokolicy (919 m) — a needle-like limestone peak
  • Alternatively: rent mountain bikes and ride the Pieniny trails
  • Visit Czorsztyn Castle ruins on the lake — 14th century fortress
  • Afternoon: swim or sail on Czorsztyńskie Lake
  • Evening: live folk music at a local tavern
🍽 Lunch at Czorsztyn Castle inn · 🍽 Dinner with folk music at Karczma UPT

Sunday 21 June

  • 7:00–7:30 AM — Morning walk on the Zdrojowy Promenade
  • 8:00 AM — Depart Szczawnica
  • 9:30 AM — Arrive Kraków
  • Fresh and relaxed, with time to unpack before brunch
🥐 Breakfast at the hotel before departure
🛌 Where to sleep: Hotel Modry Dywanik or Willa Poezja — both in the town center, walking distance to everything.
03

Dedinky & Slovak Paradise

🚗 Drive from Kraków: ~2 h · 130 km
Ladders, caves, and hidden lakes.
Slovak Paradise is not a metaphor — it's a national park of canyon gorges connected by wooden ladders and footbridges over waterfalls. Dedinky is a sleepy lakeside village at its doorstep.

Thursday 18 June

  • Depart Kraków ~2:00 PM, cross the border at Lysá nad Dunajcom
  • Arrive Dedinky ~4:00 PM, check into guesthouse
  • Walk around Palcmanská Maša lake — the "Slovak Sea"
  • Rent a paddleboat or stand-up paddleboard for the afternoon
  • Evening: stroll along the lake promenade, watch the sunset over the hills
🍽 Dinner at Koliba u Frice — hearty Slovak halušky and bryndzové pirohy

Friday 19 June

  • 7:30 AM — Drive to Suchá Belá trailhead (10 min)
  • Hike the most famous Slovak Paradise trail — a series of wooden ladders and footbridges
  • Navigate through narrow waterfalls and canyon gorges
  • ~4 hours of pure adventure photography heaven
  • Swim in the natural pools at the end of the trail
🥪 Pack picnic for the trail · 🍽 Dinner at Penzión Kamienka's terrace

Saturday 20 June

  • 9:00 AM — Guided tour of Dobšinská Ice Cave (UNESCO, dress warmly!)
  • Marvel at the 26 m-high ice walls and frozen waterfall
  • Afternoon: short hike to Tomášovský výhľad viewpoint
  • Panorama of the Slovak Paradise ridges and the lake below
  • Relax by the lake with drinks from the beach bar
🍽 Lunch at the cave restaurant · 🍽 Grilled specialities at Hotel Dedinkár

Sunday 21 June

  • 7:30 AM — Depart Dedinky
  • 9:30 AM — Arrive Kraków
  • Grab breakfast before heading home — the lake views will stay with you
🥐 Breakfast at the guesthouse before departure
🛌 Where to sleep: Hotel Dedinkár or Penzión Kamienka — cozy, family-run guesthouses right by the lake.
04

Karpacz & Góry Stołowe

🚗 Drive from Kraków: ~4 h · 320 km
Unreal rock labyrinths.
The Table Mountains look like another planet — flat-topped mesas split by narrow crevices. Karpacz offers the Wang stave church and cable car up Śnieżka, the highest peak in the Karkonosze range.

Thursday 18 June

  • Depart Kraków ~11:00 AM, drive west through Wrocław bypass
  • Arrive Karpacz ~3:00 PM, check into guesthouse
  • Visit Wang Church — a stunning 12th-century Norwegian stave church relocated here
  • Walk the town's main street, browse local craft shops
  • Evening: ride the funicular for a preview of the mountain views
🍽 Dinner at Karczma Szarotka — Silesian specialties and craft beer

Friday 19 June

  • 8:00 AM — Cable car from Karpacz up Kopa (1,377 m)
  • Ridge hike to Śnieżka (1,603 m) — the highest Karkonosze peak
  • Visit the mountaintop observatory and cross the border to Czechia
  • Stunning 360° views of both Polish and Czech sides
  • Descend via the Samotnia mountain hut for a well-earned rest
🥪 Picnic at the summit · 🍽 Dinner at Samotnia Mountain Hut

Saturday 20 June

  • 9:00 AM — Drive to Góry Stołowe National Park (50 min)
  • Explore Błędne Skały — a narrow labyrinth between towering sandstone walls
  • Continue to Szczeliniec Wielki (919 m) — the Table Mountains' highest point
  • Walk the scenic loop through otherworldly rock formations
  • Visit Kudowa-Zdrój spa town for a mineral water tasting on the way back
🍽 Lunch at Szczeliniec mountain hut · 🍽 Dinner in Karpacz at Restauracja Jamel

Sunday 21 June

  • 5:30 AM — Depart Karpacz (pack and load the car Saturday night)
  • 9:30 AM — Arrive Kraków
  • A longer drive but the moon-like Table Mountains are unforgettable
🥡 Breakfast on the road
🛌 Where to sleep: Hotel Slonek & Spa or Willa Anna — both near the town center, with mountain views.
05

Ostrava (Czech Republic)

🚗 Drive from Kraków: ~2.5 h · 180 km
Industrial grit meets cool revival.
Ostrava turned its steel mills into museums, galleries, and viewpoints. Ride an underground train at Landek Park, watch sunset from Bolt Tower, and discover fairy-tale Štramberk nearby.

Thursday 18 June

  • Depart Kraków ~2:30 PM, drive west to the Czech border
  • Arrive Ostrava ~5:00 PM, check into hotel
  • Evening at Dolní Vítkovice — a former steelworks turned cultural hub
  • Take the lift up Bolt Tower (77 m) for panoramic sunset views
  • Explore the industrial cathedral's walkways and exhibitions
🍽 Dinner at Bolt Tower restaurant with a view · Or at Stodolní Street clubs

Friday 19 June

  • 9:00 AM — Landek Park Mining Museum
  • Ride the underground train 60 m below the surface through authentic mining shafts
  • Try on a miner's helmet and see the original equipment
  • Afternoon: Ostrava Zoo (one of Czechia's largest) or Water World Sareza
  • Evening: explore Stodolní Street — over 60 bars and pubs in one street
🍽 Lunch at Landek Park's restaurant · 🍽 Traditional Czech pub dinner

Saturday 20 June

  • 10:00 AM — Day trip to Štramberk (25 km, 30 min drive)
  • Climb the Trúba tower for views of the Beskydy Mountains
  • Explore the fairy-tale wooden houses and cobblestone streets
  • Buy Štramberské uši — a local gingerbread specialty
  • Alternative: Hukvaldy Castle ruins — the largest castle complex in Moravia
🍽 Lunch at Štramberk main square · 🍽 Beer tasting at Ostrava's brewpub

Sunday 21 June

  • 7:00 AM — Depart Ostrava
  • 9:30 AM — Arrive Kraków
  • Plenty of time for a proper breakfast before the day begins
🥡 Breakfast on the road
🛌 Where to sleep: Hotel Meritum (modern, central) or Hotel Imperial (historical, elegant) — both well-located.
06

Tokaj Wine Region (Hungary)

🚗 Drive from Kraków: ~3.5 h · 200 km via Košice
UNESCO hills of sweet wine.
Volcanic slopes, centuries-old cellars carved into rock, and the legendary Tokaji Aszú. A wine tour here is a journey through underground cathedrals of barrel-aged gold. Hungarian cuisine seals the deal.

Thursday 18 June

  • Depart Kraków ~12:00 PM, drive via Rzeszów and the scenic Dukla Pass
  • Cross into Slovakia, then Hungary at Kráľovský Chlmec
  • Arrive Tokaj ~3:30 PM, check into a wine cellar guesthouse
  • Introductory tasting at a family winery — learn the 6 types of Tokaj wine
  • Evening walk along the Bodrog riverbank
🍽 Dinner at Tokaj Vintage Restaurant — duck breast with Tokaj sauce

Friday 19 June

  • 10:00 AM — Tour Disznókő Winery (architectural design + wine tasting)
  • 12:30 PM — Oremus Winery tasting in ancient cellars carved in volcanic tuff
  • 3:00 PM — Hétszőlő Winery (the oldest estate, dating to 1502)
  • Walk through the vineyard terraces with views over the Tokaj basin
  • Buy bottles directly from each cellar — they're half supermarket price
🍽 Lunch on Tokaj's main square · 🍽 Dinner at Petrács Gyógy — Hungarian goulash and lángos

Saturday 20 June

  • 10:00 AM — Drive to Sárospatak (25 km), tour the Rákóczi Castle
  • Magnificent Renaissance fortress with a hand-dug well in the courtyard
  • Afternoon: hike the Zemplén Hills — gentle trails among volcanic peaks
  • Visit a small family cellar for a last tasting
  • Evening: rustic csárda dinner with live cimbalom music
🍽 Lunch at Sárospatak · 🍽 Csárda dinner with live folk music

Sunday 21 June

  • 6:00 AM — Depart Tokaj (buy wine on Saturday to avoid Sunday closures)
  • 9:30 AM — Arrive Kraków
  • Unpack your wine haul and relive the weekend with a bottle of Aszú
🥡 Breakfast on the road
🛌 Where to sleep: Hotel Tokaj or Bordog és Kaland (wine cellar guesthouse) — authentic, unforgettable.
07

Vienna (Austria)

🚗 Drive from Kraków: ~4.5 h · 330 km via Brno
Imperial elegance, summer energy.
Schönbrunn, Hofburg, the Ringstraße — Vienna needs no introduction. In June, it's also outdoor wine taverns, Prater park, and Klimt at the Belvedere. Worth every kilometer of the drive.

Thursday 18 June

  • Depart Kraków ~10:00 AM, motorway drive via Brno
  • Arrive Vienna ~2:30 PM, check into hotel
  • Walk the Ringstraße — see the State Opera, Parliament, and City Hall
  • Visit Stephansdom cathedral and climb the south tower
  • Evening: Heurigen wine tavern in Grinzing — local wine served in a garden
🍽 Dinner at a Heurigen tavern — Wiener Schnitzel and Gemischter Satz wine

Friday 19 June

  • 9:00 AM — Schönbrunn Palace tour: 40 rooms of imperial grandeur
  • Walk the Great Parterre gardens, visit the Gloriette for city views
  • 2:00 PM — Hofburg Imperial Palace: Sisi Museum and Imperial Apartments
  • See the Spanish Riding School stables (Lipizzaner horses)
  • 7:00 PM — Prater park: ride the iconic Riesenrad Ferris Wheel at sunset
🥪 Lunch at Café Central · 🍽 Dinner at Figlmüller — famous schnitzel

Saturday 20 June

  • Choose your own adventure:
  • 🎨 Kunsthistorisches Museum (Bruegel, Vermeer, Rembrandt) OR
  • 🎨 Belvedere Gallery — Klimt's The Kiss and Schiele masterpieces
  • Alternatively: day trip to the Wachau Valley (Melk Abbey, Dürnstein ruins)
  • Evening: Naschmarkt food market for dinner
🍽 Lunch at Naschmarkt · 🍽 Final dinner at a rooftop bar

Sunday 21 June

  • 5:00 AM — Depart Vienna (pack the car on Saturday night!)
  • 9:30 AM — Arrive Kraków
  • Worth the early wake-up — Vienna never disappoints
🥡 Breakfast on the road
🛌 Where to sleep: Hotel NH Wien (good value) or Pension Liechtenstein (central). Book ahead for June!
08

Low Tatras National Park (Slovakia)

🚗 Drive from Kraków: ~3.5 h · 200 km via Poprad
Slovakia's wildest range.
The Low Tatras are rugged, uncrowded, and spectacular. Hike to Ďumbier peak for 360° ridge views, explore ice caves, and soak in thermal pools at the end of the day. True mountain escape.

Thursday 18 June

  • Depart Kraków ~1:00 PM, drive south via Poprad
  • Arrive Jasná or Liptovský Mikuláš ~4:30 PM, check in
  • Walk around Liptovský Mikuláš — a charming Liptov town
  • See the Baroque square and the Synagogue cultural centre
  • Evening stroll along the Váh River
🍽 Dinner at Koliba Repiská — bryndzové halušky and local beer

Friday 19 June

  • 6:30 AM — Early start, drive to trailhead at Trangoška
  • Hike to Ďumbier (1,843 m) — the highest peak of the Low Tatras
  • Steep climb rewards with the longest ridge-line views in Slovakia
  • ~6 h round trip, including rest and lunch on the summit
  • Descend via the same ridge or the Chopok side
🥪 Pack a hearty picnic · 🍽 Dinner at Hotel Grand Jasná

Saturday 20 June

  • 9:00 AM — Tour Demänovská Ice Cave (guided, 1 h, 5°C inside)
  • 10:30 AM — Second cave: Demänovská Cave of Freedom (different formations)
  • Afternoon: thermal pools in Liptovský Ján — natural hot springs
  • Alternatively: ride the chairlift on Jasná for panoramic views without hiking
  • Evening: sauna and relaxation at the hotel
🍽 Lunch at a Liptovský Ján restaurant · 🍽 Grill night at Penzión Bystrina

Sunday 21 June

  • 6:00 AM — Depart Jasná
  • 9:30 AM — Arrive Kraków
  • Legs will be tired but the ridge views will be worth every step
🥡 Breakfast on the road
🛌 Where to sleep: Hotel Grand Jasná or Penzión Bystrina — right at the mountain trailheads.
09

České Budějovice (Czech Republic)

🚗 Drive from Kraków: ~4.5 h · 390 km via Brno
Beer, castles, and South Bohemian charm.
The home of Budvar beer, a vast Renaissance square, and a fairy-tale castle minutes away. Český Krumlov — UNESCO-listed — is a 30-minute drive down the Vltava valley.

Thursday 18 June

  • Depart Kraków ~10:00 AM, motorway via Brno
  • Arrive České Budějovice ~2:30 PM, check in
  • 4:00 PM — Budvar brewery tour: see the bottling hall, cellars, and fermentation tanks
  • Taste unpasteurised Budvar directly from the tank
  • Walk the main square — the largest in Czechia (133 × 133 m)
🍽 Dinner at Masné Krámy — Budvar restaurant with hearty Czech cuisine

Friday 19 June

  • 9:00 AM — Drive to Český Krumlov (30 km)
  • Tour the castle: one of the largest in Central Europe
  • Walk through the old town — a UNESCO World Heritage site
  • Optional: raft the Vltava river through the town (tame rapids, 2 h)
  • Climb the castle tower for the iconic view of the red rooftops
🍽 Lunch at a Krumlov riverside restaurant · 🍽 Back in ČB for evening beer

Saturday 20 June

  • 10:00 AM — Visit Hluboká Castle (10 min from ČB)
  • A white fairy-tale castle inspired by Windsor — stunning interiors and gardens
  • Afternoon: drive to Třeboň (25 km) — historic town of fish ponds
  • Rent bikes and cycle around the Renaissance fish ponds
  • Sample local carp and Třeboň's medicinal herbs
🍽 Lunch at Hluboká or Třeboň · 🍽 Farewell dinner at ČB's main square

Sunday 21 June

  • 5:00 AM — Depart České Budějovice
  • 9:30 AM — Arrive Kraków
  • A long drive home but the fairy-tale memories make it worthwhile
🥡 Quick breakfast before leaving
🛌 Where to sleep: Hotel Zvon (directly on the main square) or Penzion U Tří sedláků — central and affordable.
