01

Solina & Bieszczady Mountains

🚗 Drive from Kraków: ~3.5 h · 180 km
Poland's wild heart.
The Bieszczady's grassy ridges roll on for miles, dotted with sheep and wooden churches. Solina Lake — the largest in Poland — mirrors it all. Boat trips, campfire evenings, and trails that make you feel miles from anywhere.
DayPlan
Thu 18 JunDrive → Solina. Walk the dam, rent a boat or kayak on the lake. Dinner with a view.
Fri 19 JunTarnica peak or Połonina Wetlińska ridge hike. Pack a picnic for the summit.
Sat 20 JunHorse-drawn cart through the hills or museum visit. Evening bonfire with oscypek.
Sun 21 JunDepart 6:00 AM → Kraków ~9:30 AM.
🛌 Where to sleep: Pensjonat pod Gwiazdami or Domki nad Soliną (cottages for 4). Book early!
02

Szczawnica & Pieniny Mountains

🚗 Drive from Kraków: ~1.5 h · 100 km
The closest mountain escape.
A charming spa town at the foot of sheer limestone cliffs. The Dunajec River Gorge raft trip is a national treasure — wooden rafts, towering rock walls, and castles overhead. Trzy Korony peak awaits hikers.
DayPlan
Thu 18 JunDrive to Szczawnica. Evening promenade walk, dinner at a local karczma.
Fri 19 JunDunajec River rafting (Sromowce → Szczawnica, ~2h). Afternoon hike to Trzy Korony.
Sat 20 JunMTB in Pieniny or hike Sokolicy. Czorsztyn Castle ruins.
Sun 21 JunMorning walk 7:00. Depart 8:00 → Kraków ~9:30.
🛌 Where to sleep: Hotel Modry Dywanik or Willa Poezja — both in town center.
03

Dedinky & Slovak Paradise

🚗 Drive from Kraków: ~2 h · 130 km
Ladders, caves, and hidden lakes.
Slovak Paradise is not a metaphor — it's a national park of canyon gorges connected by wooden ladders and footbridges over waterfalls. Dedinky is a sleepy lakeside village at its doorstep.
DayPlan
Thu 18 JunDrive to Dedinky. Walk Palcmanská Maša lake, paddleboat. Dinner at Koliba u Frice.
Fri 19 JunSuchá Belá gorge hike — ladders, cascades, photo paradise. ~4h.
Sat 20 JunDobšinská Ice Cave tour. Afternoon: Tomášovský výhľad viewpoint or lake relax.
Sun 21 JunDepart 7:30 → Kraków ~9:30. Breakfast before leaving.
🛌 Where to sleep: Hotel Dedinkár or Penzión Kamienka — cozy, family-run.
04

Karpacz & Góry Stołowe

🚗 Drive from Kraków: ~4 h · 320 km
Unreal rock labyrinths.
The Table Mountains look like another planet — flat-topped mesas split by narrow crevices. Karpacz offers the Wang stave church and cable car up Śnieżka, the highest peak in the Karkonosze range.
DayPlan
Thu 18 JunEarly start → Karpacz. Visit Wang Church, walk the town.
Fri 19 JunCable car + ridge hike to Śnieżka summit. Sunset on the mountain.
Sat 20 JunGóry Stołowe: Błędne Skały labyrinth + Szczeliniec Wielki. Unreal landscapes.
Sun 21 JunDepart 5:30 → Kraków ~9:30. Pack Saturday night.
🛌 Where to sleep: Hotel Slonek & Spa or Willa Anna — near center.
05

Ostrava (Czech Republic)

🚗 Drive from Kraków: ~2.5 h · 180 km
Industrial grit meets cool revival.
Ostrava turned its steel mills into museums, galleries, and viewpoints. Ride an underground train at Landek Park, watch sunset from Bolt Tower, and discover fairy-tale Štramberk nearby.
DayPlan
Thu 18 JunDrive → Ostrava. Evening at Dolní Vítkovice + Bolt Tower sunset.
Fri 19 JunLandek Park mining museum (underground train). Zoo or Water World Sareza.
Sat 20 JunDay trip: Štramberk fairy-tale town or Hukvaldy Castle. Czech beer tasting.
Sun 21 JunDepart 7:00 → Kraków ~9:30. Breakfast on the road.
🛌 Where to sleep: Hotel Meritum or Hotel Imperial — modern, well-located.
06

Tokaj Wine Region (Hungary)

🚗 Drive from Kraków: ~3.5 h · 200 km via Košice
UNESCO hills of sweet wine.
Volcanic slopes, centuries-old cellars carved into rock, and the legendary Tokaji Aszú. A wine tour here is a journey through underground cathedrals of barrel-aged gold. Hungarian cuisine seals the deal.
DayPlan
Thu 18 JunDrive via Rzeszów & Dukla Pass. Check into a cellar guesthouse. Evening tasting.
Fri 19 JunTour 2–3 wineries (Disznókő, Oremus, Hétszőlő). Cellar tour + tasting. Lunch on Tokaj's square.
Sat 20 JunSárospatak Castle or Zemplén Hills hike. Rustic csárda dinner with live music.
Sun 21 JunDepart 6:00 → Kraków ~9:30. Buy wine Saturday instead.
🛌 Where to sleep: Hotel Tokaj or Bordog és Kaland cellar guesthouse.
07

Vienna (Austria)

🚗 Drive from Kraków: ~4.5 h · 330 km via Brno
Imperial elegance, summer energy.
Schönbrunn, Hofburg, the Ringstraße — Vienna needs no introduction. But in June, it's also outdoor wine taverns, Prater park, and Klimt at the Belvedere. Worth every kilometer of the drive.
DayPlan
Thu 18 JunEarly start → Vienna. Walk Ringstraße, Stephansdom. Heurigen dinner.
Fri 19 JunSchönbrunn Palace + gardens. Hofburg & Sisi Museum. Prater & Riesenrad.
Sat 20 JunKunsthistorisches or Belvedere (Klimt). Or day trip to Wachau Valley.
Sun 21 JunDepart 5:00 → Kraków ~9:30. Pack everything Saturday night.
🛌 Where to sleep: Hotel NH Wien or Pension Liechtenstein — book ahead for June.
08

Low Tatras National Park (Slovakia)

🚗 Drive from Kraków: ~3.5 h · 200 km via Poprad
Slovakia's wildest range.
The Low Tatras are rugged, uncrowded, and spectacular. Hike to Ďumbier peak for 360° ridge views, explore ice caves, and soak in thermal pools at the end of the day. True mountain escape.
DayPlan
Thu 18 JunDrive to Jasná or Liptovský Mikuláš. Walk the town, dinner by the Váh.
Fri 19 JunĎumbier hike (1,843 m). Stunning ridge-line views. ~6h. Pack picnic.
Sat 20 JunDemänovská Ice Cave + Freedom Cave tours. Afternoon thermal pools.
Sun 21 JunDepart 6:00 → Kraków ~9:30. Chairlift on Saturday instead.
🛌 Where to sleep: Hotel Grand Jasná or Penzión Bystrina — at trailheads.
09

České Budějovice (Czech Republic)

🚗 Drive from Kraków: ~4.5 h · 390 km via Brno
Beer, castles, and South Bohemian charm.
The home of Budvar beer, a vast Renaissance square, and a fairy-tale castle just minutes away. Český Krumlov — UNESCO-listed — is a 30-minute drive down the Vltava valley.
DayPlan
Thu 18 JunDrive → České Budějovice. Budvar brewery tour + tasting. Dinner at Masné Krámy.
Fri 19 JunČeský Krumlov day trip: castle, Vltava rafting, Old Town. Back for beer.
Sat 20 JunHluboká Castle. Třeboň fish ponds cycling or Novohradské hike.
Sun 21 JunDepart 5:00 → Kraków ~9:30 via Brno. Quick breakfast before leaving.
🛌 Where to sleep: Hotel Zvon (on the square) or Penzion U Tří sedláků.
